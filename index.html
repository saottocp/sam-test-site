How I Got Here

The progression from accidental tinkerer to deliberate team engineer.

Phase 1 • The Spark

Dismantling old gear in a garage

Before any formal CS classes, I was the kid trying to flash custom Linux builds onto obsolete family laptops and rebuilding mountain bike suspensions with hardware-store washers. It wasn't clean work, but it killed any fear of hitting a roadblock or voiding a warranty.

Lesson carried over: If you don't know how the subsystem works, inspect the edge cases before assuming it's broken.
Phase 2 • The Reality Check

From solo hacking to team coordination at Cal Poly

Walking into university project teams quickly proved that writing clever code doesn't matter if nobody else can debug your pull request at 1 AM. Taking ownership of our team's sensor intake pipeline meant spending more time drafting clean interface specs and setting up CI workflows than writing isolated logic.

Lesson carried over: Simple, well-documented architecture beats clever, brittle solutions every time.
Phase 3 • Shipping to Prod

Production fire drills during my junior internship

Working on an internal ingest service meant real database locks, real latency regressions, and post-mortems where pointing fingers wasn't an option. I spent three months finding micro-optimizations in high-throughput pipelines and realized I enjoy back-end plumbing just as much as front-end interaction.

Lesson carried over: Measure twice, profile your queries, and run realistic benchmarks before merging.
